# ONPRO IT content guidelines

Facts and rules for anything written for onproit.com — service pages, location
pages, and blog posts. Every entry here came from a correction ONPRO IT made in
Content Review ("Request changes"), so treat it as authoritative and follow it
over anything you would otherwise assume.

The content agents read this file automatically (it is imported by CLAUDE.md).
When a correction is about ONPRO IT itself — what it offers, where it works, how
it works, pricing, claims it can or can't make — add it below as a short,
dated bullet. Don't add one-off edits that only apply to a single page.

## Facts and rules

- 2026-09-26: ONPRO IT does not take on clients that require specific
  regulatory/compliance expertise for sensitive data (e.g. medical offices,
  law firms — HIPAA and similar compliance is out of scope). Target and
  reference other business types instead: small businesses, service
  businesses, restaurants, retail, distribution, etc.
